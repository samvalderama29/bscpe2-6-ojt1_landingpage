Project Vision

To build a centralized, inspiring space that celebrates individual journeys from classrooms to corporate landscapes. By compiling BSCPE 2-6 students' OJT portfolios, this platform aims to spotlight student innovation, document experiential learning, and provide future interns with a benchmark of excellence and practical insights.
